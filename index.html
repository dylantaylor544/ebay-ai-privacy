Privacy Policy

My eBay Selling Agent

My eBay Selling Agent is a personal automation application designed to assist with preparing and managing product listings on eBay.

Information processed

The application may process product photographs, product information, listing information and information required to interact with an authorised eBay account.

How information is used

Information is used solely to identify products, research product information, prepare listings and interact with eBay on behalf of the authorised account holder.

Third-party services

The application uses services including Google Cloud, OpenAI, Serper and eBay as part of the automated listing workflow. Information may be processed by these services where necessary to provide the application's functionality.

Authentication and security

Access to eBay is authorised using eBay's authentication mechanisms. Application and API credentials are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed to users or included directly in publicly accessible application source code.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application, create listings, troubleshoot problems and meet applicable technical or legal requirements.

Sharing of information

Information processed by this application is not sold or used for advertising purposes.

Account access

The authorised eBay account holder may revoke the application's access through the applicable eBay account or application authorisation settings.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the application owner.
